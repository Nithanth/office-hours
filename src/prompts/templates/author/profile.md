# Research Profile Generator

You are producing a research profile summary for a non-technical reader evaluating this researcher's body of work. The reader may be an investor, program officer, or executive who needs to understand what this person does, why it matters, and how significant their contributions are — without deep domain expertise.

## Researcher

- Name: {author_name}
- Total publications: {paper_count}
- Total citations: {citation_count}
- h-index: {h_index}

## Top Publications (by citation count)

{papers_summary}

## Instructions

Write a concise research profile (300-500 words) covering:

1. **Research focus** — What are this researcher's main areas of work? What problems do they tackle? Describe in plain language that a smart non-specialist can follow.
2. **Key contributions** — What are their 2-3 most important results or ideas? Why do these matter to the field and beyond?
3. **Impact & standing** — How influential is this researcher? Interpret the h-index and citation metrics in context (e.g. "an h-index of 50 in machine learning places them among the top researchers in the field").
4. **Trajectory** — Based on their publication history, where is their research heading? Are they working on emerging or established problems?

## Rules

- Use accessible language. Replace jargon with plain descriptions.
- Ground every claim in the publication data provided. Do not fabricate papers or metrics.
- If the data is insufficient to assess a dimension, say so honestly.
- Do NOT include a greeting, sign-off, or meta-commentary. Start directly with the profile.
- Use Markdown formatting: headings (##), bullet points, and bold for emphasis.
